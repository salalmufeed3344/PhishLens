Cookies

PhishLens uses exactly one cookie — a security cookie that protects the check form. There is nothing to consent to beyond it.

The one cookie

  • csrftoken — Django's cross-site request forgery token. It is set when you load the check form and proves the form submission came from this site. It holds no personal data, is not used for tracking, and is not shared with anyone.

What we do not use

No analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no third-party cookies, and no preference cookies — there is nothing to remember about you between visits, because there are no accounts and nothing is stored.

Clearing it

You can clear the cookie any time in your browser settings, under cookies or site data for this site. Clearing it only means the next form submission gets a fresh token — nothing else changes.
