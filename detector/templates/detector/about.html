About PhishLens

PhishLens is an explainable phishing URL detector: paste a suspicious link, get a verdict — and the reasons behind it, in plain language.

How it works

Every check runs two detectors side by side. First, 45 features are measured from the URL string alone, and a transparent rule-based detector scores 29 named warning signs — the “@” trick, typosquats, homoglyphs, invisible characters, open-redirect laundering, file-extension domains, free-hosting logins, and more. Second, a Random Forest model trained on verified PhishTank phishing URLs and Tranco benign domains gives a phishing probability. The headline score is the average of the two. SHAP attributions from the model are translated into plain sentences, so each result explains itself.

URL-only, on purpose

PhishLens never opens, downloads, or visits the link you paste. The analysis works on the text of the address alone, which keeps the check fast and keeps you safe from the very page you are suspicious of. The trade-off is honest and stated everywhere: a phishing page hosted on a legitimate or compromised domain can still slip through, because the address itself looks clean.
