PhishLens

PhishLens analyses the URL text only. It never opens, downloads or visits the link you paste.

Results are an estimate of risk, not a guarantee — a clean-looking link can still be unsafe, and a flagged link is not proof of maliciousness. When in doubt, type the address yourself.
