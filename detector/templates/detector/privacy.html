Privacy

PhishLens is built to need as little of your information as possible. In short: there are no accounts, nothing you paste is stored, and there is no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses, and no names. You can use the whole service without telling us who you are.

The link you paste

The URL you paste is sent to this server, analysed, and discarded. It is never stored, never shared with anyone, and never opened, downloaded or visited — the analysis works on the text of the address alone. Because the link travels in the request body rather than the address bar, it does not appear in ordinary server access logs either.

Cookies

One cookie: Django's CSRF security cookie, which protects the check form from forgery. It holds no personal data and is not used for tracking. There are no analytics, advertising, or third-party cookies. See the cookies page for the details.

Analytics and tracking

None. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts, and the fonts are served from this server, so loading the page does not phone home to a font provider either.

Server logs

The host may keep brief technical logs — request times and errors — to keep the service running. Those logs do not include the URLs you paste or anything that identifies you.

Questions

There is no contact form and no support inbox. The project is open source — see the about page.
