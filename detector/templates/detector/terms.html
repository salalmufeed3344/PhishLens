Terms

PhishLens is a free service, provided as-is. These terms are short on purpose — read them in a minute.

What the verdict means

A verdict is guidance, not a guarantee. A Low verdict does not mean a link is safe, and a High verdict is not proof of maliciousness. PhishLens analyses only the text of the address — it cannot see what a page does, and it cannot catch phishing hosted on a legitimate or compromised domain. Do not rely on it alone for security decisions. When in doubt, type the address yourself, or check with the organisation directly.

No warranty

The service is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind. The maintainers are not liable for anything that happens from following — or ignoring — a verdict.

Fair use

Use the service for checking links you have a reason to check. Do not hammer it with automated requests; excessive automated use may be rate-limited to keep the service usable for everyone. Do not use PhishLens to facilitate wrongdoing.

Open source

The code is open source under the MIT licence — see the about page. These terms may change as the project develops; the version on this page is the current one.
